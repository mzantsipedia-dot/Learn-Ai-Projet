Salary Check: Product Requirements

A colourful, one-screen budgeting app that answers one question: "Will my money last until my next salary?"

From scope to detail

Scope said: "Red warning when the money is running out faster than the days."
Now we know exactly what that means: the app works out your spending pace since the last reset. If what's left won't cover that pace until your salary date, it turns red and tells you, e.g. "Running low: R500 left, 14 days until salary. At your pace you need about R2,000."
Scope said: "Set up once."
Now we know: salary, salary date, and debit orders stay until you change them. Only purchases clear, and only when you press Reset.

    Features and behaviour (with how we'll know they work)

    The salary warning: the heart of the app

    Left = salary − debit orders − purchases since reset. Pace = spending per day since reset. Red if what's left won't cover your pace until the salary date. Otherwise, a calm "on track" message.

    • R500 left, 14 days to go, fuel about R1,000/week → red, running-low message
    • One more purchase that tips the balance turns on track → red right away
    Salary and salary date

    Amount, monthly/weekly, next salary date. They stay until you change them, e.g. a raise or a new job.

    • Close and reopen: the values are still there
    • Changing them updates the status straight away
    Debit orders

    About five rows: name, amount, monthly/weekly. Never cleared by Reset.

    • They reduce "left" in the status
    • Still there after Reset
    Adding a purchase

    One compact row: amount + category (groceries, electricity, fuel, clothing, other) + Add.

    • "R600, fuel" appears in the list and spent goes up by R600
    • The fuel total adds up all fuel purchases
    Reset and reminder

    Reset clears purchases only. It never resets on its own, because salary can land late after a weekend. From the salary date onward, a reminder appears.

    • After Reset: list empty, spent R0
    • On or after salary date: reminder visible
    What-ifs (states)
    • First use: blank boxes with R signs. The status asks for salary and date first.
    • No purchases yet: shows left and days to salary, and says to add purchases to see your pace.
    • Bad amount (blank, 0, negative, letters): not added, with a friendly note asking for rands.
    • Close and reopen: everything is still there. Only Reset clears purchases.

    Look and feel

    Headings: elegant and decorative, "beautiful and patterny," like Klimt's gold. Everything else: clean and simple. Colours: gold, yellow, red, green, starry blue from The Kiss. Rands everywhere. Red is saved for warnings.

    Assumptions (confirmed by you)

    • The status sits between the debit orders and the add-a-purchase row
    • You can delete a purchase if you typed it wrong
    • Reset asks "Are you sure?" first
    • After reset, the app suggests the next salary date (one month or week later), which you can change
    • Weekly debit orders count for each week until the next salary

    Building now vs. later

    Building now

    • One screen: salary, debit orders, status, add purchase, list, reset
    • Salary warning (red / on track)
    • Reset reminder
    • Details saved between visits
    • Rands + Klimt styling

    Deferred

    • Junk-food and savings tips

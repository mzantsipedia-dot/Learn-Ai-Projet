Salary Check: Technical Blueprint

How we'll build it: a phone-friendly web app that saves your details on the phone, published free with GitHub Pages.

How it works, in plain language

HTML: the structureThe boxes, dropdowns, buttons, and headings.
CSS: the styleKlimt golds, reds, greens, and starry blues, plus decorative headings.
JavaScript: the brainsAdds up purchases, works out your pace, and decides when to turn red.

Your details are saved in local storage, a sticky note the phone's browser keeps just for this app. Nothing goes to the internet, and there are no accounts or passwords.

The salary warning maths (calc.js)

Left = salary − debit orders − purchases
Pace per day = purchases ÷ days since last salary (or last Reset)
Needed = pace × days until salary (rounded to R50)
Running low if left < needed, otherwise on track

Example: salary R20,000, debit orders R6,000, purchases R13,500 over 16 days, 14 days to go.
Left = R500. Pace ≈ R844/day. Needed ≈ R11,800. So it's red: "Running low: R500 left, 14 days until salary. At your pace you need about R11,800."

Before your first Reset, "days since last salary" counts back one month (or week) from your salary date, so setting up mid-month still works.

If things go wrong

  • Storage blocked (private mode): the app still works, with a note that details won't be saved
  • Bad amount: "Please enter an amount in rands, like 450."
  • Salary date today or past: the reset reminder shows

Kept simple on purpose

  • Web app, not an app-store app (no fees or approval)
  • Phone storage, not online accounts
  • Fixed five debit-order rows
  • Simple overall pace, not per-category forecasting
